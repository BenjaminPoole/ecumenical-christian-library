Sermons of John Wesley

Sep 05, 20264 min read

All 141 sermons of the 1872 Jackson edition (Sermons on Several Occasions), numbered as in Thomas Jackson’s Works. Text from the Wesley Center Online. Sermons 1–141 are the complete 1872 corpus; sermons 142–151 in some modern editions come from Outler’s later critical edition and are not part of the 1872 text.
