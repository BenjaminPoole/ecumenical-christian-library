Christian Leaders of the Last Century

Christian Leaders of the Last Century

Oct 10, 20261 min read

Ryle’s biographical sketches (1868) of the leaders of the eighteenth-century Evangelical Revival in England and Wales.

Text from Grace Gems (modernised), except the Whitefield chapter, taken from Ryle’s separately published sketch (Project Gutenberg). Grace Gems does not carry the chapter on Augustus Toplady, which is therefore absent here.
