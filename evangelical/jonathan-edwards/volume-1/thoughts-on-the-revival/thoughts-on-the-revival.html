SOME THOUGHTS
CONCERNING
THE PRESENT REVIVAL OF RELIGION
IN NEW ENGLAND,
AND THE
WAY IN WHICH IT OUGHT TO BE ACKNOWLEDGED AND PROMOTED.
HUMBLY OFFERED TO THE PUBLIC,
IN A TREATISE ON THAT SUBJECT.
—Prepare ye the way of the Lord, make straight in the desert a high-way for our God
