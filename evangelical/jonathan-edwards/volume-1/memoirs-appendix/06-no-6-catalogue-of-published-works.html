APPENDIX, NO. VI.

APPENDIX, NO. VI.

Sep 05, 20262 min read

Catalogue of President Edwards’s Works, Heretofore Published

  1. God glorified in Man’s Dependence; A Sermon on 1 Cor. i. 29-31. Boston.

  2. A Divine and Supernatural Light imparted to the Soul by the Spirit of God; A Sermon on Matt. xvi. 17. Boston

  3. (Probably.) Curse ye Meroz; A Sermon on Judges v. 3. (This I have not found.)

  4. Narrative of Surprising Conversions. London.

  5. Five Discourses prefixed to the first American edition of the preceding. Boston.

  6. Sinners in the hands of an angry God; A Sermon on Deut. xxxii. 35. Boston.

  7. Sorrows of the Bereaved spread before Jesus; A Sermon at the Funeral of the Rev. William Williams, on Matt. xiv. 12. Boston.

  8. Distinguishing Marks of a Work of the True Spirit; A Sermon on 1 John iv. 1.preached at New-Haven, Sept. 10. 1741. Boston.

  9. Thoughts on the Revival of Religion in New England in 1740. Boston.

  10. The Watchman’s Duty and Account; A Sermon on Heb. xiii. 17. at the Ordination of the Rev. Jonathan Judd. Boston.

  11. The True Excellency of a Gospel Minister; A Sermon on John v. 35. At the Ordination of the Rev. Robert Abererombie. Boston.

  12. Treatise on Religious Affections. Boston.

  13. True Saints, when absent from the Body, present with the Lord; A Sermon on .2 Cor. v. 8. At the Funeral of Rev. David Brainerd. Boston.

  14. God’s awful Judgments in breaking the Strong Rods of Community; A Sermon on the Death of Col. John Stoddard. Boston.

  15. Life and Diary of the Rev. David Brainerd. Boston.

  16. Christ the Example of Gospel Ministers; A Sermon on John xiii. 15, 16. At the Ordination of the Rev. Job Strong. Boston.

  17. Qualifications for Full Communion in the Visible Church. Boston.

  18. Farewell Sermon to the People of Northampton. Boston.

  19. True Grace distinguished from the Experience of Devils; A Sermon on James ii. 19. Before the Synod of Newark. New York.

  20. On the Freedom of the Will. Boston.

  21. On Original Sin. Boston.

  22. Eighteen Sermons, annexed to the Life by Dr. Hopkins. Boston.

  23. The History of Redemption. Edinburgh.

  24. Nature of Virtue. Boston.

  25. God’s Last End in the Creation. In the same pamphlet as the preceding.

  26. Practical Sermons. Edinburgh.

  27. Twenty Sermons. Edinburgh.

  28. Miscellaneous Observations. Edinburgh.

  29. Miscellaneous Remarks. Edinburgh.
