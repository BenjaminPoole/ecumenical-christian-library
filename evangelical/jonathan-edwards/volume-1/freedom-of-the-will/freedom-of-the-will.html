A Careful and Strict Inquiry into the Prevailing Notions of the Freedom of the Will

A Careful and Strict Inquiry into the Prevailing Notions of the Freedom of the Will

17544 min read

A
CAREFUL AND STRICT INQUIRY
INTO THE
MODERN PREVAILING NOTIONS
OF THAT
FREEDOM OF WILL
WHICH IS SUPPOSED TO BE ESSENTIAL TO MORAL AGENCY, VIRTUE. AND VICE, REWARD AND PUNISHMENT, PRAISE AND BLAME.
Rom. ix. 16 it is not him that willeth.
