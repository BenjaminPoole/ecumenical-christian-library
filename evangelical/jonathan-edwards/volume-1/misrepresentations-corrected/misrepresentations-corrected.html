MISREPRESENTATIONS CORRECTED,
AND
TRUTH VINDICATED,
IN
A REPLY
TO THE
REV. MR. SOLOMON WILLIAMS’S BOOK,
ENTITLED,
THE TRUE STATE OF THE QUESTION CONCERNING THE QUALIFICATIONS NECESSARY TO LAWFUL COMMUNION IN THE CHRISTIAN SACRAMENTS.
Prov. xii. 17.He that speaketh truth, showeth forth righteousness.
Chap. xxii. 20, 21. Have I not written to thee excellent things in counsels and knowledge; that I might make thee know the certainty of the words of truth, that thou mightest answer the words of truth to them that send unto thee?
