The Works of Jonathan Edwards

The Works of Jonathan Edwards

Oct 10, 202621 min read

The two-volume Works of Jonathan Edwards (Banner of Truth edition, revised and corrected by Edward Hickman, 1834), from the CCEL text. Volume 1 opens with the Memoirs of Jonathan Edwards by Sereno E. Dwight, followed by the major treatises; Volume 2 contains the sermons, the shorter treatises, the Life and Diary of David Brainerd, and the biblical notes. Public domain.
