Sermons on Proverbs

c. 1855–1892 (preached across Spurgeon's ministry)2 min read

Spurgeon’s sermons on texts from the book of Proverbs, gathered as a volume by the Christian Classics Ethereal Library. The sermons themselves were preached across his ministry and published in the weekly pulpit series.
