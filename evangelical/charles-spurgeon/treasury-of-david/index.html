The Treasury of David

1865–18852 min read

Spurgeon’s monumental commentary on the Psalms — twenty years in the writing (1865–1885). For each Psalm: an Exposition, Explanatory Notes and Quaint Sayings gathered from many authors, and Hints to the Village Preacher. Text from The Spurgeon Archive.
