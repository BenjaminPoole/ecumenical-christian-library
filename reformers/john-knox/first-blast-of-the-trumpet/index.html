The First Blast of the Trumpet Against the Monstrous Regiment of Women

15581 min read

Knox’s notorious 1558 tract against female rule, aimed at Mary Tudor and Mary of Guise. Its publication months before Elizabeth I’s accession permanently soured Knox’s relations with the English crown. Edward Arber’s 1878 edition.
