The History of the Reformation of Religion in Scotland

The History of the Reformation of Religion in Scotland

15872 min read

Knox’s own narrative of the Scottish Reformation, written between 1559 and his death in 1572 and first published in full in 1587. The principal contemporary source for the events it describes, and by a leading actor in them.
