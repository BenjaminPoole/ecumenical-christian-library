Commentary on the Heidelberg Catechism

Commentary on the Heidelberg Catechism

158410 min read

Ursinus’s lectures on the Heidelberg Catechism, of which he was principal author, compiled after his death by his student David Pareus and first published in 1584 - the standard sixteenth-century exposition of the catechism and Ursinus’s magnum opus. Translated by G. W. Willard (Columbus, Ohio, 1852); text from the Monergism compiled edition of that translation. One note per catechism question. Questions 33, 61, 84-85 and 95 carry no separate marker in this edition; their exposition stands within the neighbouring questions’ notes.
