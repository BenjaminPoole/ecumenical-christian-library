Selected Works of Huldreich Zwingli

Selected Works of Huldreich Zwingli

1522-15262 min read

Nine documents of Zwingli’s Zurich reformation - including the Acts of the First and Second Zurich Disputations, the Sixty-seven Articles, and his defences against the charge of heresy - translated for the first time under Samuel Macauley Jackson (Philadelphia, 1901).

  1. Editor’s Preface and Introduction (S. M. Jackson)
  2. I. Letter of Huldreich Zwingli to Erasmus Fabricius About the Proceedings, on The
  3. II. Petition of Certain Preachers of Switzerland to the Most Reverend Lord Hugo, Bishop of Cons
  4. III. Acts of the Convention Held in the Praiseworthy City of Zurich on The, Part 1
  5. III. Acts of the Convention Held in the Praiseworthy City of Zurich on The, Part 2
  6. III. Acts of the Convention Held in the Praiseworthy City of Zurich on The, Part 3
  7. IV. Ordinance and Notice. How Ma
  8. V. Refutation of the Tricks of the Baptists by Huldreich Zwingll, Part 1
  9. V. Refutation of the Tricks of the Baptists by Huldreich Zwingll, Part 2
  10. V. Refutation of the Tricks of the Baptists by Huldreich Zwingll, Part 3
  11. V. Refutation of the Tricks of the Baptists by Huldreich Zwingll, Part 4
  12. V. Refutation of the Tricks of the Baptists by Huldreich Zwingll, Part 5
