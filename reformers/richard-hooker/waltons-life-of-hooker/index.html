The Life of Mr. Richard Hooker

The Life of Mr. Richard Hooker

16652 min read

Izaak Walton’s classic biography of Hooker (1665), printed as the standard companion to the Works since the seventeenth century. Included here as supporting material: its author is Walton, not Hooker. Text from Keble’s 7th edition via the Online Library of Liberty.
