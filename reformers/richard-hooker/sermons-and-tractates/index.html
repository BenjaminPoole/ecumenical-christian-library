Sermons and Tractates

1585-16001 min read

Hooker’s surviving sermons and the documents of the Temple controversy with Walter Travers, from volume III of Keble’s edition. A Learned Discourse of Justification is kept as its own work in this collection and not repeated here.

  2. Travers’ Supplication to the Council
  3. Answer to Travers’ Supplication
  4. A Learned Sermon of the Nature of Pride
  5. A Remedy against Sorrow and Fear
  6. Two Sermons upon Part of St Jude - Dedication
  7. Two Sermons upon St Jude 1
  8. Two Sermons upon St Jude 2
  9. A Sermon found among the Papers of Bishop Andrews
