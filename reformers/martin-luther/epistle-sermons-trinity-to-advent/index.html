Epistle Sermons, Vol. 3: Trinity Sunday to Advent

Epistle Sermons, Vol. 3: Trinity Sunday to Advent

15442 min read

Luther’s sermons on the Epistle texts from Trinity Sunday to Advent, from his Church Postil. Translated under John Nicholas Lenker (1909). One note per sermon; each sermon carries Lenker’s topical section captions.

  1. Trinity Sunday - Romans 11, 33-36
  2. First Sunday After Trinity - 1 John 4, 16-21
  3. Second Sunday After Trinity - 1 John 3, 13-18
  4. Third Sunday After Trinity - 1 Peter 5, 5-11
  5. Fourth Sunday After Trinity - Romans 8, 18-22
  6. Fifth Sunday After Trinity - 1 Peter 3, 8-15
  7. Sixth Sunday After Trinity - Romans 6, 3-11
  8. Seventh Sunday After Trinity - Romans 6, 19-23
  9. Eighth Sunday After Trinity - Romans 8, 12-17
  10. Ninth Sunday After Trinity - 1 Corinthians 10, 6-13
  11. Tenth Sunday After Trinity - 1 Corinthians 12, 1-11
  12. Eleventh Sunday After Trinity - 1 Corinthians 15, 1-10
  13. Twelfth Sunday After Trinity - 2 Corinthians 3, 4-11
  14. Thirteenth Sunday After Trinity - Galatians 3, 15-22
  15. Fourteenth Sunday After Trinity - Galatians 5, 16-24
  16. Fifteenth Sunday After Trinity - Galatians 5, 25-26 and 6, 1-10
  17. Sixteenth Sunday After Trinity - Ephesians 3, 13-21
  18. Seventeenth Sunday After Trinity - Ephesians 4, 1-6
  19. Eighteenth Sunday After Trinity - 1 Corinthians 1, 4-9
  20. Nineteenth Sunday After Trinity - Ephesians 4, 22-28
  21. Twentieth Sunday After Trinity - Ephesians 5, 15-21
  22. Twenty First Sunday After Trinity - Ephesians 6, 10-17
  23. Twenty Second Sunday After Trinity - Philippians 1, 3-11
  24. Twenty Third Sunday After Trinity - Philippians 3, 17-21
  25. Twenty Fourth Sunday After Trinity - Colossians 1, 3-14
  26. Twenty Fifth Sunday After Trinity - 1 Thessalonians 4, 13-18
  27. Twenty Sixth Sunday After Trinity - 2 Thessalonians 1, 3-10
  28. Twenty Seventh Sunday After Trinity - 2 Peter 3, 3-7

Overlap with other editions

Same text elsewhere in this library

The sermon for the Twelfth Sunday After Trinity (2 Cor. 3:4–11) also appears, split into sections 14–25, in CCEL’s Assorted Sermons — see Sermons of Martin Luther. The same is true of the Twenty-Third Sunday After Trinity (Phil. 3:17–21), held there as sections 28–35.
