Works of Martin Luther (Philadelphia Edition) - further treatises

Works of Martin Luther (Philadelphia Edition) - further treatises

1517-15221 min read

Treatises from volumes I-II of the Philadelphia (Holman) edition not otherwise in this collection: the prefaces, Baptism, Confession, the Fourteen of Consolation, the New Testament (Holy Mass), the Papacy at Rome, the Blessed Sacrament, the Ban, the Brief Explanation, and the Eight Wittenberg Sermons. Works already present elsewhere (Ninety-five Theses, Good Works, Christian Nobility, Babylonian Captivity, Christian Liberty) are not repeated. Each work opens with its editor’s historical introduction.

  1. Prefaces to the Collected Editions
  2. A Treatise on Baptism
  3. A Discussion of Confession
  4. The Fourteen of Consolation, Part 1
  5. The Fourteen of Consolation, Part 2
  6. A Treatise on the New Testament, that is, the Holy Mass, Part 1
  7. A Treatise on the New Testament, that is, the Holy Mass, Part 2
  8. The Papacy at Rome, Part 1
  9. The Papacy at Rome, Part 2
  10. The Papacy at Rome, Part 3
  11. The Blessed Sacrament of the Holy and True Body of Christ, and the Brotherhoods
  12. A Treatise concerning the Ban
  13. A Brief Explanation of the Ten Commandments, the Creed, and the Lord’s Prayer
  14. The Eight Wittenberg Sermons, Part 1
  15. The Eight Wittenberg Sermons, Part 2
