Martin Luther (1483–1546)

Martin Luther (1483–1546)

Sep 05, 20261 min read

Augustinian friar and professor at Wittenberg whose Ninety-five Theses of 1517 began the Reformation. His recovery of justification by faith alone, his German Bible, and his catechisms shaped Protestantism more than any other single figure.
