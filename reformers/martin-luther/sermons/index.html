Overlap with other editions

Same text elsewhere in this library

Sections 14–25 are the Lenker translation of Luther’s sermon on 2 Corinthians 3:4–11, which also appears whole as Twelfth Sunday After Trinity in Epistle Sermons: Trinity to Advent. Sections 28–35 are likewise his sermon on Philippians 3:17–21, held whole as Twenty-Third Sunday After Trinity. Both editions are kept: this one is CCEL’s Assorted Sermons (split by section), the other Gutenberg’s Epistle Sermons (whole sermons).
