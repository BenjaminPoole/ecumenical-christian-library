Commentary on Genesis (Chapters 1-9)

15442 min read

Luther’s commentary on Genesis chapters 1-9, from his lectures: vol. 1 Luther on the Creation (Gen 1-4) and vol. 2 Luther on Sin and the Flood (Gen 4-9), translated under John Nicholas Lenker (1904-10). The remaining chapters of Luther’s vast Genesis lectures have no public-domain English translation.
