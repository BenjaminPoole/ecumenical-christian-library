The Augsburg Confession

The Augsburg Confession

15302 min read

The confession of faith presented to the Emperor Charles V at the Diet of Augsburg on 25 June 1530 — the foundational confessional document of the Lutheran Reformation. Drafted by Melanchthon (with Luther’s approval, though Luther was confined at Coburg and could not attend), it sets out the evangelical faith in 28 articles. Translated by F. Bente and W. H. T. Dau for the Triglot Concordia (1921).
