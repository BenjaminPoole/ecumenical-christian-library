Apology of the Augsburg Confession

Apology of the Augsburg Confession

15311 min read

Melanchthon’s Apologia Confessionis Augustanae (1531), written to defend the Augsburg Confession against the Roman Confutatio. It is the longest of the Lutheran confessional writings and Melanchthon’s most sustained theological work. Translated by F. Bente and W. H. T. Dau for the Triglot Concordia.
