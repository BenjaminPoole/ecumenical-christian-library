For the life appeared, and we have seen, and bear witness, and shew unto you that everlasting life, which was with the Father, and appeared unto us.

IN that St John saith, “The thing which was from the beginning,” and “the everlasting life that was with the Father,” he witnesseth that Christ is very God; as he doth in the beginning of his gospel, saying: The word,” or the thing?, was at the beginning, and the thing was with Godand that thing was God, and all things were made by it.”

And when he saith, Which we heard, and saw with our eyes, and our hands handled” him; he testifieth that Christ is very man also; as he doth in the beginning of his gospel saying: “The word,” or that thing, “was made flesh,” that is, became man. And thus we have in plain and open words a manifest article of our faith, that our Saviour Christ is very God and very man.

Which article whosoever not only believeth, but also believeth in it, the same is the son of God, and hath everlasting life in him, and shall never come into condemnation: as it is written, John i. He gave them power to be the sons of God, in that they believed in his name;” and John iii., He that believeth in the Son hath everlasting life; and a little before in the said chapter, He that believeth in him shall not be condemned.” And to believe in the words of this article is that eating of Christ’s flesh, and drinking his blood, [8 As the Hebrew word 137 means alike word and thing, Tyndale was led to consider the apostle as using Aóyos for its equivalent.]
