Expositions and Notes on Sundry Portions of the Holy Scriptures

Expositions and Notes on Sundry Portions of the Holy Scriptures

1531-15362 min read

Parker Society vol. 2 (1849), ed. Henry Walter: Tyndale’s exposition of Matthew v-vii - the Sermon on the Mount - with its prologue, split by chapter; his exposition of the First Epistle of John, with its introductory notice, prologue, and text split by chapter; his marginal notes on Matthew i-xxi; and The Practice of Prelates, with its own introductory notice and preface. (This volume does not in fact contain A Pathway into the Holy Scripture - that appears only in passing reference here; it is not among the pieces actually printed in this volume.)

  1. Prologue to the Exposition of Matthew V, VI, and VII
  2. Exposition of Matthew V, VI, and VII: The Fifth Chapter
  3. Exposition of Matthew V, VI, and VII: The Sixth Chapter
  4. Exposition of Matthew V, VI, and VII: The Seventh Chapter
  5. Introductory Notice and Prologue to the Exposition of the First Epistle of St John
  6. Exposition of the First Epistle of St John: Chapter I
  7. Exposition of the First Epistle of St John: Chapter II
  8. Exposition of the First Epistle of St John: Chapter III
  9. Exposition of the First Epistle of St John: Chapter IV
  10. Exposition of the First Epistle of St John: Chapter V
  11. Marginal Notes on the First Twenty-One Chapters of St Matthew’s Gospel
  12. Introductory Notice and Preface to the Practice of Prelates
  13. The Practice of Prelates, Part 1
  14. The Practice of Prelates, Part 2
  15. The Practice of Prelates, Part 3
