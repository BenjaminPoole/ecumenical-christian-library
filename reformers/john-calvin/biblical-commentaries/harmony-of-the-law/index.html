Harmony of the Law

Harmony of the Law

15634 min read

Calvin’s four-volume harmony of Exodus, Leviticus, Numbers, and Deuteronomy (CCEL calcom03–06), translated by Charles William Bingham for the Calvin Translation Society. Deviation from the per-Bible-chapter format: Calvin arranged these books topically — a narrative ‘History’ thread plus the Law ordered by the Ten Commandments — so this folder follows the source’s own division structure: one note per source division, numbered continuously across the four volumes and grouped below by the source’s section headings. Exodus–Deuteronomy therefore have no chapter-by-chapter notes; each note’s title names the passage(s) it covers.
