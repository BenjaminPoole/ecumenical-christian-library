John Calvin (1509–1564)

John Calvin (1509–1564)

Sep 05, 20261 min read

French reformer and theologian of Geneva, whose Institutes, biblical commentaries, and tracts shaped Reformed Protestantism. This collection gathers his major works in the classic 19th-century English translations of the Calvin Translation Society, converted from CCEL ThML transcriptions and (for some treatises) archive.org scans of the printed volumes.

Collections

  • Institutes of the Christian Religion — Calvin’s systematic theology in four books (1559 final edition, Beveridge translation); 80 chapters plus prefatory material.
  • Biblical Commentaries — the 45-volume Calvin Translation Society edition: commentaries or lectures on most of the Bible, one note per chapter, plus the Harmony of the Law and the Harmony of the Evangelists.
  • Treatises — shorter works: the Reply to Sadoleto, Short Treatise on the Lord’s Supper, Treatise on Relics, The Necessity of Reforming the Church, the Geneva Catechism, and On the Christian Life.
