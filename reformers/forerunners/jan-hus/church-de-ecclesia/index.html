The Church (De Ecclesia)

The Church (De Ecclesia)

14131 min readHussite

Written: Prague (Bethlehem Chapel) (modern Prague, Czech Republic), 1402–1415. Open in Google Maps

Hus’s principal work, written in 1413 - the doctrine of the church that drew directly on Wycliffe and led to his condemnation at Constance. Translated with notes by David S. Schaff (New York, 1915).
