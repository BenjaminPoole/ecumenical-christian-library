Religion therefore took her name ὦ religando, of tying together, and binding into the Lord’s bundle®.” And Augustine in his book de Quantitate Anime, chapter 36, saith: “True religion is, whereby the soul tieth herself through reconciliation to one God, from whom through sin she had as it were broken away®.” The same Augustine, in his book de Vera Religione, and last chapter, saith: “Let religion tie us unto one God Almighty, whereof it is believed to be named religion’.”
