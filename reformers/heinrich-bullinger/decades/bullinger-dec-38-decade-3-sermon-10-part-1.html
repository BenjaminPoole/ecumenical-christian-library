original sin And this evil verily, this corruption and this sin, although ’ it lie hid in infants, and by reason of their tender age doth not break forth into any deed-doing, yet notwithstanding it is a sin; and such a sin verily as maketh them endangered unto God’s wrath, and separateth them from the fellowship of God. For with the most holy God, who ” is a consuming fire,” no man can abide but he that is unspotted and clean
