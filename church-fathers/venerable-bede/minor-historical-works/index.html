Minor Historical Works

Sep 05, 20262 min read

Bede’s shorter historical and hagiographical works, together with the eyewitness letter on his death. Translations from J. A. Giles’s Minor Historical Works of Venerable Bede (1843), except the death-letter (from Sellar’s edition). These are converted from uncorrected OCR of the 1843 print, so occasional character errors and stray printed side-notes may remain in the text.

  • Life and Miracles of St Cuthbert — Bede’s prose Life of St Cuthbert of Lindisfarne (46 chapters).
  • Lives of the Holy Abbots of Wearmouth and Jarrow — The Historia Abbatum — lives of Benedict Biscop, Ceolfrid, and their successors.
  • Life of St Felix — Bede’s prose paraphrase of Paulinus of Nola’s verse life of St Felix of Nola.
  • Life of St Vedast — Bede’s abridgement of the life of St Vedast (Vaast) of Arras.
  • Letter of Bede to Abbot Albinus — A short covering letter sending the completed Ecclesiastical History to Albinus.
  • Letter of Bede to Bishop Egbert — Bede’s last major work (734): a pastoral letter to Egbert of York on church reform.
  • Of the Holy Places — De Locis Sanctis, a description of Jerusalem and the holy sites, abridged from earlier writers.
  • Letter of Cuthbert on the Death of Bede — Cuthbert’s eyewitness account of Bede’s death (735), including Bede’s Death Song. By Bede’s pupil, not Bede himself.
  • The Seven Wonders of the World — De septem mundi miraculis — printed by Giles among Bede’s works but generally regarded as spurious (pseudo-Bede).
