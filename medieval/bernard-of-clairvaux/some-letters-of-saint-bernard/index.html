Some Letters of Saint Bernard

Some Letters of Saint Bernard

1119–11534 min read

Bernard of Clairvaux — translated by Samuel J. Eales, with a preface by Francis Aidan Gasquet, from CCEL.

Sixty-six letters to popes, kings, bishops, abbots, monks and noblewomen — the abbot of Clairvaux acting on the whole of twelfth-century Europe from a monastery. Letter 60 carries his indictment of Abelard’s teaching.
