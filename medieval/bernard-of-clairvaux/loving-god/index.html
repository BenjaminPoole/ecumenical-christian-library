On Loving God

On Loving God

c. 11282 min read

Bernard of Clairvaux — from CCEL.

Bernard’s answer to the question why and how much God is to be loved: “The reason for loving God is God Himself; the measure, to love without measure.” It traces four degrees of love, from loving oneself for one’s own sake to loving oneself only for God’s.
