Explanation of the Rule of St Augustine

c. 11251 min read

Hugh of St Victor — Explanation of the Rule of St Augustine, translated by Aloysius Smith (London: Sands & Co., 1911), read from the Internet Archive scan.

A commentary on the Rule that governed the canons regular of St Victor, and through them much of twelfth-century religious life: what it means to have one heart and one soul, to hold nothing as one’s own, and to be corrected without bitterness.
