Jean Gerson

Jean Gerson

Sep 22, 20261 min read

Jean Gerson (1363–1429), chancellor of the University of Paris and the leading conciliarist of the Great Schism, who argued at Constance that a general council stands above a disputed pope. He wrote as much on the discernment of spirits and the spiritual life of ordinary people as on church politics.
