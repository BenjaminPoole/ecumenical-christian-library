Historia Calamitatum

Historia Calamitatum

c. 11322 min read

Peter Abelard — translated by Henry Adams Bellows, with an introduction by Ralph Adams Cram (Saint Paul: Thomas A. Boyd, 1922), from CCEL.

The Story of My Misfortunes, written as a letter of consolation to a friend: Abelard’s account of his own rise as a logician, his rivalry with William of Champeaux and Anselm of Laon, his love for Heloise and its punishment, the burning of his book at Soissons, and his flight from one enmity to the next.
