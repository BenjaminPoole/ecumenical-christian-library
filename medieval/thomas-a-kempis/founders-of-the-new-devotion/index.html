The Founders of the New Devotion

The Founders of the New Devotion

c. 14204 min read

Thomas à Kempis — translated from the Latin by J. P. Arthur (London: Kegan Paul, 1905), from CCEL.

Lives of Gerard Groote, Florentius Radewin and their followers — the Devotio Moderna of the Low Countries, the movement in whose houses Thomas à Kempis was raised and out of which the Imitation came.
