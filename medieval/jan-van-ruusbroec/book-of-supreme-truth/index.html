The Book of Supreme Truth

c. 13501 min read

Jan van Ruusbroec — translated from the Flemish by C. A. Wynschenk Dom, edited by Evelyn Underhill (London: J. M. Dent, 1916), from CCEL.

Ruusbroec’s last work, written to clear up misunderstandings of what he had said about union with God — that the creature never becomes God, but is united to Him without intermediary.
