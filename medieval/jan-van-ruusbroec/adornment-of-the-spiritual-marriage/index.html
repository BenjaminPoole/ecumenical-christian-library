The Adornment of the Spiritual Marriage

The Adornment of the Spiritual Marriage

c. 13353 min read

Jan van Ruusbroec — translated from the Flemish by C. A. Wynschenk Dom, edited with an introduction and notes by Evelyn Underhill (London: J. M. Dent, 1916), from CCEL.

Ruusbroec’s masterpiece, called in Flemish Die Chierheit der gheestelijker Brulocht — the Spiritual Espousals — on the three lives of the Christian: active, inward, and contemplative, all of them the answer to Behold, the Bridegroom cometh; go ye out to meet Him. Printed here with The Sparkling Stone and The Book of Supreme Truth.
