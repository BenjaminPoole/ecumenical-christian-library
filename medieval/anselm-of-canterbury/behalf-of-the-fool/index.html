In Behalf of the Fool

In Behalf of the Fool

c. 10782 min read

Gaunilo of Marmoutiers and Anselm of Canterbury — translated by Sidney Norton Deane (Open Court, 1903; reprint 1926), from CCEL.

The first and most famous objection to the ontological argument, with its answer. Gaunilo, a monk of Marmoutiers, writes on behalf of the fool of Psalm 14 — if the argument worked, he says, it would prove a perfect lost island into existence. Anselm replies that the argument holds only of that than which nothing greater can be conceived.
