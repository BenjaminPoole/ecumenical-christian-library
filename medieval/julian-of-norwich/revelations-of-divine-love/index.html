Revelations of Divine Love

Revelations of Divine Love

c. 13932 min read

Julian of Norwich — from CCEL.

The sixteen shewings granted to an anchoress of Norwich on 8 May 1373, as she lay expecting to die, and the twenty years of meditation she gave them: the hazelnut that is all that is made, the motherhood of God, and the answer she was given about sin — that all shall be well, and all shall be well, and all manner of thing shall be well.
