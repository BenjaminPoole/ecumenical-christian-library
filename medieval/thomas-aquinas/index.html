Thomas Aquinas

Thomas Aquinas

Sep 05, 20261 min read

St. Thomas Aquinas (c. 1225–1274), Dominican friar, priest, and Doctor of the Church. This collection holds his two great theological summaries in public-domain / freely-available English translations.

Works

  • Summa Theologiae — his masterwork (1265–1274), in five Parts: 611 Questions, some 2,600 Articles. Trans. Fathers of the English Dominican Province (1920); public domain, from New Advent.
  • Summa Contra Gentiles — the earlier apologetic summa (c. 1259–1265), in four Books, 462 chapters. Hanover House translation (1955–57), ed. Joseph Kenny O.P., from isidore.co.
