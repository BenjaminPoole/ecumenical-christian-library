15292 min read

Der Kleine Katechismus (1529), written after Luther’s visitation of the Saxon churches revealed widespread ignorance of the faith. Translated by Robert E. Smith from the German text of the Triglot Concordia; this translation is freely distributed by Project Wittenberg rather than being in the public domain.
