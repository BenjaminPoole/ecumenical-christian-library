Constantinople III (680)

Constantinople III (680)

680–6811 min read

6th Ecumenical Council · Constantinople · A.D. 680–681

The sixth ecumenical council: condemned Monothelitism, affirming two wills in Christ.

Translated by Henry Percival, Nicene and Post-Nicene Fathers, Second Series, Vol. 14 (1900). Source: New Advent. Public domain.
