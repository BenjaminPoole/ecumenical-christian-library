Westminster Assembly (1643)

1643–16531 min read

Assembly of Divines · Westminster · A.D. 1643–1653

The assembly of divines convened by the English Parliament to reform the Church of England. It produced the Westminster Standards — the Confession of Faith and the Larger and Shorter Catechisms — which became the doctrinal standards of English-speaking Presbyterianism.

The three standards are in the vault, under Reformation Confessions.

Documents

  • The Westminster Confession of Faith (1646)
  • The Westminster Larger Catechism (1648)
