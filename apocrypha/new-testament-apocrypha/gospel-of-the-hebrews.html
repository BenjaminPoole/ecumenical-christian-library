Gospel of the Hebrews

Gospel of the Hebrews

c. 100–15014 min readadapted from the Internet ArchiveHereticalNew Testament Apocrypha

Written: Alexandria, Egypt, c. 100-150. Open in Google Maps

Fragment 1. Irenaeus, Against Heresies i. 26. 2

But the Ebionites use only that Gospel which is according to Matthew, and repudiate the Apostle Paul, calling him an apostate from the Law.

Fragment 2. Irenaeus, Against Heresies iii. 11. 7

For the Ebionites, who use only that Gospel which is according to Matthew, are convicted out of that very book as not holding right views about the Lord.

Fragment 3. Clement of Alexandria, Stromateis i. 9. 45

Even (or also) in the Gospel according to the Hebrews is written the saying, ‘he that wondereth shall reign, and he that reigneth shall rest’.

Fragment 4. Clement of Alexandria, Stromateis v. 14. 96

For those words have the same force as these: He shall not cease from seeking until he find, and having found, he will be amazed, and having been amazed will reign, and having reigned will rest.

Fragment 5. Origen, On John ii. 12

And if any accept the Gospel according to the Hebrews, where the Saviour himself saith, ‘Even now did my mother the Holy Spirit take me by one of mine hairs, and carried me away unto the great mountain Thabor’, he will be perplexed, &c… .

Fragment 6. Origen, On Jeremiah, homily xv. 4

And if any one receive that saying, ‘Even now my mother the Holy Spirit took me and carried me up unto the great mountain Thabor’, and the rest… .

Fragment 7. Eusebius, Ecclesiastical History iii. 39. 17

[Speaking of the early writer Papias:] He has also set forth (or expounded) another story, about a woman accused of many sins before the Lord, which the Gospel according to the Hebrews also contains.

Fragment 8. Eusebius, Ecclesiastical History iv. 22. 8

Hegesippus made use in his Memoirs of the Gospel according to the Hebrews.

Fragment 9. Eusebius, Ecclesiastical History iii. 25. 5

[In his list of antilegomena, writings whose canonicity was disputed:] And among them some have placed the Gospel according to the Hebrews which is the especial delight of those of the Hebrews who have accepted Christ.

Fragment 10. Eusebius, Ecclesiastical History iii. 27. 4

[The Ebionites repudiated Paul] and used only the Gospel called according to the Hebrews, making but slight account of the others.

Fragment 11. Eusebius, Theophany iv. 12 (preserved in Syriac)

As we have found somewhere in the Gospel which the Jews have in the Hebrew tongue, where it is said: I choose for myself them that are good (or well pleasing): the good are they whom my Father which in heaven giveth (or hath given) me.
