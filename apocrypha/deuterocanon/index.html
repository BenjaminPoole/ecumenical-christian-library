Deuterocanon

Deuterocanon

Nov 25, 20262 min readDeuterocanon

The Jewish books of the Greek Old Testament that stand outside the Hebrew Bible, received as Scripture by the Catholic and Orthodox churches and printed as the Apocrypha in Protestant Bibles.

Most are given in the King James Version of eBible.org; 3 and 4 Maccabees and Psalm 151 are from Sir Lancelot Brenton’s translation of the Septuagint. All are in the public domain. Entries run in rough order of composition.

  • Letter of Jeremiah — Letter ridiculing the idols of Babylon, printed as Baruch 6 in the KJV.
  • Tobit — Pious Naphtalite exile Tobit, his son Tobias and the angel Raphael, with the tale of Sarah and the demon Asmodeus.
  • Baruch — Prophetic book of confession, wisdom and consolation set among the Babylonian exiles.
  • Sirach — Wisdom book of Jesus ben Sira: proverbs on wisdom, conduct and the praise of famous men.
  • 1 Esdras — Return from exile and rebuilding of the Temple under Cyrus and Darius, with the tale of the three guardsmen and Ezra’s reforms.
  • Additions to Esther — Greek additions to Esther: Mordecai’s dream, the royal edicts and the prayers of Mordecai and Esther.
  • Bel and the Dragon — Daniel exposes the idol Bel and destroys the dragon.
  • Judith — Jewish widow who beheads the Assyrian general Holofernes and saves Bethulia.
  • Prayer of Azariah and Song of the Three Holy Children — The three young men praise God in the fiery furnace (Greek addition to Daniel 3).
  • Prayer of Manasseh — Penitential prayer of the Judean king Manasseh in captivity.
  • Psalm 151 — Supernumerary psalm of David on his anointing and victory over Goliath.
  • Susanna — Daniel exposes two lecherous elders who falsely accuse Susanna.
  • 2 Maccabees — Jewish revolt against Antiochus IV under Judas Maccabeus, with the martyrs and the story of Heliodorus.
  • 1 Maccabees — Jewish revolt against Antiochus IV Epiphanes under Judas Maccabeus and his brothers.
  • 3 Maccabees — Persecution of the Egyptian Jews under Ptolemy IV Philopator and their deliverance.
  • Wisdom of Solomon — Greek wisdom book in Solomon’s name praising Wisdom, righteousness and immortality.
  • 4 Maccabees — Philosophical discourse on the supremacy of devout reason, illustrated by the martyrdom of Eleazar and the seven brothers.
  • 2 Esdras — Apocalypse of Ezra on the fall of Jerusalem and the end of the age (includes 4 Ezra, chs 3–14, with Christian chs 1–2 and 15–16).
