Jubilees

Jubilees

c. 160–150 BC1 min readadapted from pseudepigrapha.comHereticalOT Pseudepigrapha

Written: Jerusalem, c. 160-150 BC. Open in Google Maps

The Book of Jubilees, or the Little Genesis, retells Genesis 1 to Exodus 12 as an angelic revelation to Moses on Mount Sinai, dating events by jubilees of forty-nine years. R. H. Charles’s translation, divided into five parts.

  • Chapters 1–10
  • Chapters 11–20
  • Chapters 21–30
  • Chapters 31–40
  • Chapters 41–50
