Testaments of the Twelve Patriarchs

Testaments of the Twelve Patriarchs

c. 2nd century BC (with later Christian interpolations)1 min readadapted from New AdventHereticalOT Pseudepigrapha

Written: Antioch (modern Antakya, Turkey), c. 2nd century BC - 2nd century AD. Open in Google Maps

The deathbed addresses of the twelve sons of Jacob to their children, each recalling the patriarch’s own life, warning against a particular vice and urging a particular virtue, and ending with a prophecy and a command for burial. Translated by Robert Sinker for the Ante-Nicene Fathers, volume 8.

  • Testament of Reuben — on Thoughts.
  • Testament of Simeon — on Envy.
  • Testament of Levi — on the Priesthood and Arrogance.
  • Testament of Judah — on Fortitude, and Love of Money, and Fornication.
  • Testament of Issachar — on Simplicity.
  • Testament of Zebulun — on Compassion and Mercy.
  • Testament of Dan — on Anger and Lying.
  • Testament of Naphtali — on Natural Goodness.
  • Testament of Gad — on Hatred.
  • Testament of Asher — on Two Faces of Vice and Virtue.
  • Testament of Joseph — on Sobriety.
  • Testament of Benjamin — on a Pure Mind.
