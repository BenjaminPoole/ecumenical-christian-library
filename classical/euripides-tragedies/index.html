Euripides — Tragedies

Euripides — Tragedies

5th century BC1 min read

All nineteen surviving plays of Euripides (eighteen tragedies and the one complete satyr play, the Cyclops), in complete public-domain English translations.

The core ten come from Theodore Alois Buckley’s collected Tragedies of Euripides, Volume I (prose); the remaining nine are drawn from the verse translations of Gilbert Murray, Arthur S. Way, and Edward P. Coleridge (via Project Gutenberg and Wikisource). Each play file names its own translator and source.

Collected volume

  • The Tragedies of Euripides, Vol. I (Buckley) — Hecuba · Orestes · Phoenissae · Medea · Hippolytus · Alcestis · Bacchae · Heracleidae · Iphigenia in Aulis · Iphigenia in Tauris

Individual plays

  • Andromache (Way)
  • Cyclops (Way)
  • Electra (Murray)
  • Helen (Way)
  • Heracles (The Madness of Heracles) (Way)
  • Ion (Coleridge)
  • Rhesus (Murray)
  • The Suppliants (Coleridge)
  • The Trojan Women (Murray)
