Systematic Theology

1871–18731 min read

Charles Hodge — New York: Charles Scribner and Company, 1871–1873, from the CCEL text of the 1940 Eerdmans reprint.

The summa of Old Princeton: fifty years of teaching at Princeton Theological Seminary set out in three volumes — an Introduction on theological method and the rule of faith, then Theology Proper, Anthropology, Soteriology and Eschatology. Hodge’s theologian is to be “governed by the same rules as a man of science”: the Scriptures contain the facts, and theology arranges them.
