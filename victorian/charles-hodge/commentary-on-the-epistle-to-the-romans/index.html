Charles Hodge — A Commentary on the Epistle to the Romans, a new edition, revised and in great measure rewritten (Philadelphia: William S. & Alfred Martien, 1864; this printing Alfred Martien, 1873).

Hodge’s commentary on Romans, first published in 1835 and here rewritten, the book through which he taught Paul’s doctrine of justification to two generations of Princeton students. An introduction on Paul, the church at Rome and the plan of the epistle is followed by the sixteen chapters, each taken section by section: a summary of contents, an analysis of the argument, a verse-by-verse commentary weighing the Greek text and the interpreters from the Fathers to Tholuck and De Wette, and the doctrines and practical remarks the passage teaches.
