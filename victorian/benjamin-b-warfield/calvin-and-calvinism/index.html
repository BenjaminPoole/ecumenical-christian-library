Calvin and Calvinism

19311 min read

Benjamin B. Warfield — Calvin and Calvinism, volume V of his works (New York: Oxford University Press, 1931). Published in 1931, it is in the public domain in the United States; the committee’s prefatory note and the other editorial matter of the volume may still be in copyright elsewhere.

Warfield’s articles on Calvin and Calvinism, the fifth volume of his works: the sketch of Calvin’s life and work (John Calvin: The Man and his Work), the three long studies of the Institutes (the knowledge of God, the doctrine of God, the doctrine of the creation), the article Calvinism from the New Schaff-Herzog Encyclopedia, and the history of the Institutes from the Memorial Edition. Chapters 3 and 4 are the book’s text of two essays first printed in the Princeton Theological Review (1909): Calvin’s Doctrine of the Knowledge of God and Calvin’s Doctrine of God. Calvin as a Theologian, the 1909 address, is not in this volume.
