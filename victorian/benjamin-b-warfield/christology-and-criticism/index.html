Christology and Criticism

19291 min read

Benjamin B. Warfield — Christology and Criticism, volume III of his works (New York and London: Oxford University Press, 1929). Published in 1929, it is in the public domain in the United States; the committee’s prefatory note and the other editorial matter of the volume may still be in copyright elsewhere.

Warfield’s historico-critical articles on the person and work of Christ: the divine Messiah in the Old Testament, the people’s misconception of Jesus and the charge of blasphemy, Jesus’ alleged confession of sin, the article on Jesus Christ, Schmiedel’s “pillar-passages”, the “two natures” and recent christological speculation, “Christless Christianity” (against Drews), the twentieth-century Christ, the essence of Christianity and the cross, and an appendix on the supernatural birth of Jesus.
