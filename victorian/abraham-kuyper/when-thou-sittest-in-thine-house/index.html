When Thou Sittest in Thine House

18993 min read

Abraham Kuyper — When Thou Sittest in Thine House: Meditations on Home Life, translated from the Dutch by John Hendrik de Vries (Eerdmans, 1929). Published in 1929, it is in the public domain in the United States only (the 95-year term ended in 2025); it may still be in copyright elsewhere.

Fifty-eight meditations on the Christian household: the family’s place in God’s creation, husband and wife, parents and children, brothers and sisters, the home’s work, money and trouble, sickness and old age, the family’s dead, and the generations. The foreword is dated Amsterdam, 1 July 1899.
