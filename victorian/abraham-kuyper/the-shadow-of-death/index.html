In the Shadow of Death

18934 min read

Abraham Kuyper — In the Shadow of Death: Meditations for the Sick-Room and at the Death-Bed, translated from the Dutch by John Hendrik de Vries (Grand Rapids: Wm. B. Eerdmans, 1929). Published in 1929, it is in the public domain in the United States (the 95-year term ended in 2025); it may still be in copyright elsewhere.

Forty-eight meditations, In de schaduwe des doods (1893), written for De Heraut first for others on whose way Kuyper saw that death had cast its shadow, and finished, as his preface says, after he had wept at the grave of a child of his own. They go from divine chastisement, the body and the physician, through the sick-bed, sleepless nights, the dying-bed and the last hour, to the widow, the grave, the seeing again of those who have died, and being “clothed upon”. Each meditation is headed by its text of Scripture.
