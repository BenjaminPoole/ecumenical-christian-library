The South-African Crisis

19001 min read

Abraham Kuyper — The South-African Crisis, translated from the Dutch by A. E. Fletcher (London, 1900). Published in 1900, it is in the public domain.

Kuyper’s essay on the origins of the Boer War, first published in the Revue des Deux Mondes in February 1900 and dated Amsterdam, 15 January 1900. Writing as a Dutch statesman and Calvinist who had long admired Britain, he argues from the history of the Cape and the Transvaal that the British Government was wrong to settle its quarrel with the South African republics by force rather than by arbitration.
