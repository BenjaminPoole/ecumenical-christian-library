His Decease at Jerusalem

19002 min read

Abraham Kuyper — His Decease at Jerusalem: Meditations on the Passion, translated from the Dutch by John Hendrik de Vries (1928). Published in 1928, it is in the public domain in the United States only (the 95-year term ended in 2023); it may still be in copyright elsewhere.

Fifty meditations on the sufferings and death of Christ, from the decease He was to accomplish at Jerusalem, through Gethsemane, the trial, the cross and the burial, to the blood of Abel. The foreword is dated Amsterdam, November 1900.
