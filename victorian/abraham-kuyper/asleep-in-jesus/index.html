Asleep in Jesus

Asleep in Jesus

19023 min read

Abraham Kuyper — Asleep in Jesus: Meditations on Death and the Life Beyond, translated from the Dutch by John Hendrik de Vries (1929). Published in 1929, it is in the public domain in the United States (the 95-year term ended in 2025); it may still be in copyright elsewhere.

Fifty-two meditations on the state of the believer who has died, each taken from a text of Scripture: the dead in Christ, their clothing, their service and rest before God, the judgment, and the resurrection. The foreword is dated The Hague, 29 October 1902.
