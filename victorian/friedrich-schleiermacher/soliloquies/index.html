Friedrich Schleiermacher — Soliloquies, translated with an introduction and notes by Horace Leland Friess (Chicago: Open Court, 1926).

Schleiermacher’s Monologen of 1800, five reflective addresses to himself (Reflection, Soundings, The World, Prospect, Youth and Age) on the freedom and individuality of the moral life, with the translator’s preface and introduction, Schleiermacher’s prefaces to the second and third editions, his later revisions of the text, and an appendix on the development of his philosophical system and his conception of a philosopher-priest, with a bibliography. US public domain only (1926).
