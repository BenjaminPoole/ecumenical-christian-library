The Life of Schleiermacher

18602 min read

Friedrich Schleiermacher — The Life of Schleiermacher, as Unfolded in his Autobiography and Letters, translated by Frederica Rowan (London: Smith, Elder, 1860), two volumes.

Frederica Rowan’s translation of Schleiermacher’s Autobiography and Letters (London, 1860, two volumes), as published by his family: his own sketch of his childhood, his parents’ letters, his years with the Moravians, at Halle and as a tutor, his letters from Landsberg, Berlin, Potsdam and Stolpe (volume 1), then Halle, his love for Henriette von Willich, his marriage and family life in Berlin, the wars, his journeys, and his death in 1834 (volume 2). The book prints no chapter titles; the notes follow its running heads.
