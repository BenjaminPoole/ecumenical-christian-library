1806–18342 min read

Friedrich Schleiermacher — Selected Sermons of Schleiermacher, translated by Mary F. Wilson (London: Hodder and Stoughton; New York: Funk & Wagnalls, 1890), from CCEL.

Twenty-seven sermons from Schleiermacher’s ministry at Halle and at the Dreifaltigkeitskirche in Berlin, with a biographical sketch by the translator. The third was preached in 1813, in the war of liberation against Napoleon.
