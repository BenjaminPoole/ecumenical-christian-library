Introductions to the Dialogues of Plato

18041 min read

Friedrich Schleiermacher — Introductions to the Dialogues of Plato, translated by William Dobson (Cambridge: J. & J. J. Deighton, 1836).

Schleiermacher’s general introduction to Plato and his introductions to each dialogue, in three parts (Part I: the Phaedrus, Lysis, Protagoras and the dialogues grouped with them, with the Apology, Crito and Ion; Part II: the Gorgias, Theaetetus and the dialogues to the Banquet, Phaedon and Philebus, with the Theages, Erastae, Alcibiades I, Menexenus, Larger Hippias and Clitophon; Part III: the Republic, with the Timaeus and Critias), followed by the translator’s notes.
