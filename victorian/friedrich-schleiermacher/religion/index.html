On Religion

17991 min read

Friedrich Schleiermacher — On Religion: Speeches to Its Cultured Despisers, translated with an introduction by John Oman (London: Kegan Paul, Trench, Trübner, 1893), from CCEL.

Five speeches addressed to the educated Romantics of Berlin who had turned their backs on religion: religion is neither metaphysics nor morals but “a sense and taste for the Infinite”. The book from which Protestant liberal theology is usually dated. Oman translated the third edition (1821) with Schleiermacher’s later explanations, and gives the changes from the first edition in an appendix.
