Gottfried Wilhelm Leibniz — translated by E. M. Huggard (1951), from Project Gutenberg.

The only book Leibniz published in his lifetime, written against Pierre Bayle’s argument that the evil in the world cannot be reconciled with a God at once good and almighty. Its answer is that this is the best of all possible worlds — not that it holds no evil, but that no other whole would be better, the evil in it counted. Voltaire wrote Candide against it. Public domain in the US only.
