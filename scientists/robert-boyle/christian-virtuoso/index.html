The Christian Virtuoso

The Christian Virtuoso

16901 min read

Robert Boyle — EEBO-TCP A28945, keyed from the 1690 edition.

Boyle’s answer to the charge that experimental philosophy makes men irreligious: that it does the opposite, since the man who has looked hardest at the contrivance of things is the last to think them accidental. Printed in 1690 with two pieces subjoined — a discourse on the distinction between what is above reason and what is against it, and Greatness of Mind Promoted by Christianity.
