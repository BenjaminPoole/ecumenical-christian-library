A Free Discourse Against Customary Swearing

A Free Discourse Against Customary Swearing

c. 16471 min read

Robert Boyle — EEBO-TCP A28981, keyed from the 1695 edition.

Written in Boyle’s twenties and published in the year of his death by John Williams. It answers, one at a time, the thirteen things men say in excuse of the oaths they swear without meaning them, and ends in six directions for breaking the habit. A Dissuasive from Cursing is printed with it.
