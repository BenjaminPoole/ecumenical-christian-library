Five Essays on Religion

Five Essays on Religion

1597–16251 min read

Francis Bacon — EEBO-TCP B11307, keyed from The Essayes or Counsels, Civill and Morall (1625), the last edition in Bacon’s lifetime.

The five of Bacon’s fifty-eight Essays or Counsels whose subject is religion, gathered here under a title of the vault’s own, from the final edition of 1625: on truth, where Pilate jests and would not stay for an answer; on death; on the unity of the church, and when zeal becomes a scandal; on atheism, where a little philosophy inclineth man’s mind to atheism, but depth in philosophy bringeth men’s minds about to religion; and on superstition, which Bacon thought worse than unbelief.
