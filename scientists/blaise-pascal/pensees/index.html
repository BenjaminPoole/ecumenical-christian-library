Blaise Pascal — translated by W. F. Trotter (1910), from Project Gutenberg.

The notes for an apology for the Christian religion that Pascal did not live to write, found after his death in 1662 in bundles pinned together and printed by Port-Royal eight years later. They are given here in Brunschvicg’s arrangement and W. F. Trotter’s translation, in the fourteen sections and under the numbering by which they are cited — the wager is 233, the thinking reed 347, and the heart with its reasons 277.
