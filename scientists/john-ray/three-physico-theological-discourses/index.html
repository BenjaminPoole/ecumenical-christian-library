Three Physico-Theological Discourses

Three Physico-Theological Discourses

16933 min read

John Ray — EEBO-TCP A58184, keyed from the 1693 edition.

Three discourses on the earth’s own history: the creation of the world, the general deluge — with the question of what fossils are, which Ray was among the first to answer correctly — and the dissolution of the world by fire. They are the beginning of geology in English as much as they are theology, and Ray keeps returning to the same difficulty: how long the evidence in the rocks needs, against how long Scripture seems to allow.
