A Persuasive to a Holy Life

A Persuasive to a Holy Life

17001 min read

John Ray — EEBO-TCP A58177, keyed from the 1700 edition.

Ray’s last book, and the only one of his that is straightforwardly practical divinity: that a holy life is also the happy one, argued through every good men actually pursue — health, safety, riches, pleasure, honour, friends — and then through the happiness of the inward man and of the life to come.
