John Henry Newman

John Henry Newman (1801–1890), the leader of the Oxford Movement, received into the Catholic Church in 1845, cardinal in 1879, and canonised in 2019. His works are here in the uniform edition: the Parochial and Plain Sermons and the Oxford University Sermons, The Arians of the Fourth Century, the Lectures on Justification, the Essay on the Development of Christian Doctrine, The Idea of a University, the Apologia pro Vita Sua (both texts), the Grammar of Assent, the two novels, The Dream of Gerontius and the rest of the verse, and the essays, sketches, tracts and controversial writings. Text from the Newman Reader.
