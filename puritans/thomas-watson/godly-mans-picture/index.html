The Godly Man's Picture

The Godly Man's Picture

16662 min read

Thomas Watson — from the Text Creation Partnership’s transcription of the 1666 edition, keyed by hand from the page images.

The Godly Man’s Picture, Drawn with a Scripture Pencil asks who the godly man is and answers it in twenty-four characteristical signs, each given a section of its own here, before turning to use, exhortation and comfort. Two sermons printed with it — on the bruised reed and on my beloved is mine — close the book. Spelling and punctuation are as printed in 1666. A few words the transcribers could not read, and the Greek and Hebrew their transcription does not carry, are marked […].
