An Heavenly Conversation

An Heavenly Conversation

16492 min read

Jeremiah Burroughs — from the Text Creation Partnership’s transcription of the 1652 Two Treatises of Mr. Jeremiah Burroughs, keyed by hand from the page images.

The second of the two treatises, preached on Philippians 3:20, our conversation is in heaven. Burroughs opens what it is for the saints to be citizens of heaven and to trade there, gives seven evidences of it and four reasons for it, and ends with seven rules for getting such a conversation. Its companion is A Treatise of Earthly-Mindedness. Spelling and punctuation are as printed in 1652.
