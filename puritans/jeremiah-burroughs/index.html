Jeremiah Burroughs

Jeremiah Burroughs

Oct 10, 20261 min read

Jeremiah Burroughs (1599–1646), preacher at Stepney and Cripplegate and one of the five Dissenting Brethren of the Westminster Assembly, was known for pressing the sweetest subjects in the plainest words. Richard Baxter said that if all the Independents had been like Burroughs the breach would never have widened. He died at forty-six, and most of what survives was printed from his hearers’ notes.

Works (4)

  • The Rare Jewel of Christian Contentment — Eleven sermons on Philippians 4:11, and the classic Puritan treatment of contentment. (13 notes)
  • A Treatise of Earthly-Mindedness — On Philippians 3:19: what it is to mind earthly things, and how the heart is got free. (8 notes)
  • An Heavenly Conversation — Its companion, on Philippians 3:20: the saints as citizens of heaven. (22 notes)
  • Of Walking with God — On Genesis 5:24, printed with the two treatises above. (9 notes)
