Of Walking with God

Of Walking with God

16491 min read

Jeremiah Burroughs — from the 1652 Two Treatises of Mr. Jeremiah Burroughs.

A third treatise bound with the two on earthly-mindedness and heavenly conversation, preached on Genesis 5:24, and Enoch walked with God. Burroughs opens what walking with God is in nine particulars, sets out twelve excellencies and ten evidences of it, gives twelve rules of direction, and answers the objection of God hiding his face. Spelling and punctuation are as printed in 1652.
