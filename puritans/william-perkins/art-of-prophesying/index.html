The Art of Prophesying

The Art of Prophesying

16072 min read

William Perkins — translated by Thomas Tuke from Perkins’ Latin Prophetica (1592) and printed in 1607; from the Text Creation Partnership’s transcription of that edition, keyed by hand from the page images.

The first Protestant manual of preaching in English, and the book that shaped the Puritan pulpit: the preacher is to read the text, give the sense, gather the doctrine, and apply it to the seven kinds of hearer. Perkins died in 1602, seven years before this translation; he is the elder to whom the Puritan divines in this section trace their method.

Spelling is as printed in 1607, where u and v, and i and j, are each still one letter — so vnto, haue and iudge are unto, have and judge. Note titles use the modern letterforms.
