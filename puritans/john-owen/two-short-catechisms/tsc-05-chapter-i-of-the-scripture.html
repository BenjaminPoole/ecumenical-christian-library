Chap. I. — Of the Scripture.

Ques. 1. What is Christian religion?

Ans. The 1only way of2 3 knowing God aright, and 4living unto him.

Q. 2. Whence is it to be learned?

A. From the holy5 Scripture only. Isa. viii. 20; John v. 39.

Q. 3. What is the Scripture?

A. The books of the ^aOld and ^bNew6 7 8 Testament, ^cgiven by inspiration from God, containing all things necessary to be believed and done, that God may be worshipped and our souls saved. ^aIsa. viii. 20; Rom. iii. 2. ^bRev. xxii. 19, 20. ^c2 Tim. iii. 16, 17; Ps. xix. 7, 8; Jer. vii. 13; John xx. 31.

Q. 4. How know you them to be the word of God?

A. By the 9testimony10 of God’s Spirit, working faith in my heart to close with that 11heavenly majesty, and clear divine truth, that shineth in them.
