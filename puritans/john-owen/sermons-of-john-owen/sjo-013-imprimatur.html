Imprimatur

c. 1646–1683 (various occasions, published posthumously)1 min read

Die Mercurii, 31 Januarii 1648.

Ordered by the Commons assembled in Parliament, That Mr Allen do give the thanks of this House to Mr Owen for the great pains he took in his sermon preached before the House this day at Margaret’s, Westminster; and that he be desired to print his sermon at large; wherein he is to have the like privilege of printing it as others in the like kind usually have had.

Hen. Scobell, Cler. Parl. Dom. Com.
