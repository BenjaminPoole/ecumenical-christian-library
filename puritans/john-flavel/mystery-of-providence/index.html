The Mystery of Providence

The Mystery of Providence

16782 min read

John Flavel — read again from the page images of the 1840 Presbyterian Board of Publication edition (archive.org), printed as Divine Conduct, or The Mystery of Providence, in modern spelling.

Flavel preaches Psalm 57:2 — I will cry unto God most high, unto God that performeth all things for me — and the argument runs in five general heads: that the saints’ affairs are conducted by a special providence, what providence has actually performed for them (in ten particulars, from their formation in the womb to the mortification of their corruptions), that observing it is a duty, how to observe it, and why. Five practical cases close it, and four shorter pieces are printed after.

Three of them — The Righteous Man’s Refuge, Christian Resignation and Encouragements to Trust in God — are the 1840 editor’s additions from Flavel’s other works, and are kept here as that edition prints them.
